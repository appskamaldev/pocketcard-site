Pocket Card support

Business cards for Apple Wallet, in English and Arabic.

Contact

Questions, problems or ideas? Email appskamaldev@gmail.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version.

Common questions

  • “Add to Apple Wallet” fails: check your internet connection and try again. Adding a card needs a connection; editing doesn’t.
  • My card shows the classic layout, not the poster: the full-artwork poster style needs iOS 27 in Wallet.
  • Update a card: edit it in Pocket Card, then tap Update in Wallet.

Privacy

Your cards are stored on your iPhone. There is no account, no ads, no tracking and no analytics. Read the privacy policy.
