Privacy policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

What Pocket Card stores

The cards you make: names, job title, company, phone, email, website, address, QR code content, colours, artwork, photos and logos, plus unfinished drafts.

Where it is stored

Your cards are stored on your iPhone. There is no sign-up or login, and the developer cannot see your stored cards. Pocket Card does not use iCloud sync.

Adding a card to Apple Wallet

Apple requires every Wallet pass to be signed with the developer’s certificate. When you tap Add to Apple Wallet or Update in Wallet, the app sends that card’s details and images over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) to Pocket Card’s signing service, which runs on Netlify. The service signs the pass, sends it straight back to your iPhone, and keeps no copy: it doesn’t save the card’s details or images, and it doesn’t log them. Netlify, the hosting provider, may keep standard technical logs such as IP addresses and request times under its own privacy policy. After that, the pass is held by Apple Wallet on your device.

Other people on your cards

You can make cards for other people, for example colleagues in an Organization, and fill a card from Contacts, which brings the contact’s photo too. Their contact details and photos are stored only on your iPhone. They are sent to the signing service only to make the card, when you add it to Wallet or tap Share Card, and they are not stored there. Share as Image and Share as PDF are made on your iPhone and send nothing until you choose where to share the file.

QR codes

If you choose a contact QR code, it contains the contact details you entered, and anyone who scans your card can read them. Only add details you’re happy to share.

Permissions

Camera access is used only to scan a QR code you want to put on your card. The system contact picker gives the app only the contacts you pick; the app never asks for access to all your contacts. Photos you choose with the system photo picker are used for artwork and logos; the app doesn’t get access to the rest of your library. Logo background removal runs on your device with Apple Vision. Manage permissions in Settings.

Analytics and crash reports

Pocket Card includes no analytics or advertising SDK. Apple may share anonymous, aggregated crash and usage statistics with the developer from people who chose to share them in iPhone Settings.

No tracking or ads

There are no ads or tracking in Pocket Card, and the developer does not sell or share your data.

Children

Pocket Card is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices and deleting data

Delete a card in the app to remove it from the app. Remove a pass from Apple Wallet in Wallet itself. Deleting the app removes all of its cards from your iPhone. Nothing is kept on the signing service, so there is nothing to delete there.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page if the policy changes and revise the last-updated date.
